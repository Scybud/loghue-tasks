Three things it does well

Schedule it, repeat it, and see it on a calendar.

Every task has a deadline, so your list always knows what's late, what's today, and what can wait.

Schedule

Give a task a deadline, plus an optional start and end time so the hours it takes are blocked out on your calendar.

Repeat

Make a task recurring and each occurrence gets its own entry. Finish today's without touching tomorrow's.

Calendar

Switch from the list to a calendar by year, month, week, day, or hour. Overdue tasks turn red and finished ones fade out.

Reminders

Hear about it before the deadline, not after.
